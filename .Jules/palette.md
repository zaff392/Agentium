## 2024-07-04 - Chat Webview Form Control Accessibility\n**Learning:** In the Agentium extension's chat webview, custom form controls (like select and textarea) lack visual labels. They also rely heavily on hover states, making them difficult for screen reader users and keyboard navigators.\n**Action:** Always add descriptive `aria-label`s to custom form controls without visual labels, and explicitly style `:focus-visible` states to match existing hover states for clear keyboard navigation.
